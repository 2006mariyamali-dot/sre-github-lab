
# Customer Service & Medical Equipment Maintenance Management System 
## Overview
ElectroMed Corporation deals with the sales, installation, repair, servicing, and maintenance 
of medical equipment. Its service operations involve customers, technicians, workshop staff, 
accounts, store staff, and management. Currently, information is maintained through physical 
records, Excel files, quotations, invoices, and other separate records. 
The proposed system will centralize customer service and medical equipment maintenance 
information to make service operations easier to manage and monitor.
## Scope
- Customer Management 
- Equipment Management 
- Service Request Management 
- Technician Management 
- Maintenance and Repair Management
- Out of scope: 
- Full accounting and financial management
- Payroll/HR management 
- Patient medical records 
## Stakeholders
- Management : Monitor service activities and performance
- Service Team : Manage and track customer complaints
- Technicians : Access equipment details and record repairs
- Sales Team : Access customer and equipment information   
## Functional requirements
- FR-01: The system shall let a student create a task with a title.
- FR-02: The system shall display the student's recorded tasks.
- FR-03: The system shall let a student mark a task as complete.
## Nonfunctional requirements
- NFR-01: For 100 stored tasks, the task list shall appear within
 2 seconds in at least 95 of 100 trials on the agreed test device.
- NFR-02: All task actions shall be operable using a keyboard
 alone, verified against each documented task workflow.
## Acceptance criteria
- Customer, equipment, and service information is maintained in a centralized system 
instead of being dependent on scattered records.  
- Service staff can locate the required customer and equipment information more easily 
than with the existing separate records.  
- Service requests can be monitored clearly so that pending and completed requests are 
identifiable.  
- Technicians and service staff can access the service history required for equipment 
maintenance and repair activities.  
- Management can obtain a clear view of ongoing and completed service activities.  
- The organization reduces its dependence on physical records and separate Excel files 
for routine customer service and equipment maintenance activities.
